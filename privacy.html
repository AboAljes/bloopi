Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy covers the Bloopi YouTube channel and "Kids Shorts Bot", the private tool its owner uses to publish videos to that channel.

Viewers

We do not collect, store or share any personal information about people who watch our videos. Our videos are published as "Made for Kids". On those videos, YouTube turns off comments and personalised ads. YouTube's own data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The upload tool

Kids Shorts Bot uses YouTube API Services, and only to upload videos to the channel owner's own YouTube channel. It is used only by the channel owner. It does not access anyone else's account or data.

  • What it accesses: permission to upload and manage videos on the owner's channel.
  • What it stores: a sign-in token, kept privately by the owner, and a list of past episode titles.
  • What it shares: nothing. No data is sold or shared with third parties.

By using it, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings.

Contact

Questions: please contact us through the "About" page of the Bloopi channel on YouTube.
